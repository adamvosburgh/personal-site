*Full Disclosure: The Edge of Information Design* opens at MoMA on 27 September 2026 and runs through 13 June 2027. It is the museum's first exhibition given entirely to information design, and it sits in the street-level gallery, which is free.

*Two Sides of the Same Coin* is in as a printed disk, paired with an interactive touchscreen visualization. Made with Laura Kurgan and Dan Miller.

Also in the show: Kate Crawford and Vladan Joler's *Calculating Empires*, Forensic Architecture's *Seizure of the Iuventa*, Giorgia Lupi and Stefanie Posavec's *Dear Data*, Fernanda Viégas and Martin Wattenberg's *History Flow*, and Ed Hawkins's *Warming Stripes*.

Organized by Paola Antonelli, with Jules Bernstein and Forrest Pelsue.
