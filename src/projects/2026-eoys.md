A shared 3D model of the exhibition space, editable by everyone at once. Built for the MS CDP 2026 End of Year Show and used by students to coordinate with each other, and as a common reference between the facilities and audio-visual offices.

Show planning usually happens through a mix of floor plans, emails, and people standing in a room pointing. None of those hold a current state that everyone can see. This does: students place and move their own work in the model, and what facilities and AV are looking at is the same thing the students are editing.

The rooms are scanned rather than drawn. Scans are processed through a layout pipeline into GLB models that the server imports on startup, so the space in the browser is the space as it actually is, including the things that are in the way. Assets are uploaded through an admin panel and approved before they enter the shared library.

Built with Three.js and Yjs for real-time sync, on a Node and SQLite backend, deployed with Docker behind a Cloudflare tunnel. It will be extended for the coming academic year.
